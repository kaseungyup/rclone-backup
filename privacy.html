Privacy policy

tim-rclone-backup is a personal tool used only by its owner. It requests the Google Drive drive.file scope, so it can access only the files it creates in the owner's own Google Drive.

It collects no data from other users. It sends no data to the developer or to any third party. All data stays in the owner's own Google Drive.

Contact: open an issue at github.com/kaseungyup/rclone-backup.
